# Workshop abstract summary

**Title:** Topology Optimization Utilizing VAE Latent Vector

**Program:** 2024 Summer/Fall KAIST Undergraduate Research Program (URP)

**Researcher:** Jaeuk Kim, Mechanical Engineering, KAIST

**Advisor:** Ikjin Lee

**Teaching assistant:** Jihwan Park

The workshop abstract presents VAE compression and k-nearest-neighbor
interpolation as a way to predict structural designs for new conditions from
previously optimized examples. It describes a 10,000-topology base dataset and
uses predicted/reference compliance ratios to assess the generated designs.

Its stated conclusion is that the method shows promise, with many ratios near
one, while prediction quality degrades with larger neighbor counts. It discusses
latent-space nonlinearity and possible overfitting as explanations.

This is a summary of the abstract's claims. The [final-study summary](final-study.md)
provides the settings and context; the [project history](../docs/PROJECT_HISTORY.md)
explains why the archive also contains reinforcement-learning prototypes.
